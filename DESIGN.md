# Seams Familiar design

## North Star

An orderly workwear catalog with the personality of instant prints and hand-marked shop tags. Put the garment, fit, price, and shop in view before asking the shopper to open details.

## Color and type

The Scout stylesheet owns the runtime palette. White paper, plum ink, and violet controls define the browsing surface. The public All decades view uses a plain white catalog. The 80s view uses four original photographic instant-print frames (ivory, taped, worn, and narrow pearl) and a vertical multicolor film stripe. Each aperture contains the seller photo, with spacing below for the price tag. The 90s view uses an uninterrupted black-and-white checkerboard along the left edge and the purple-and-teal zigzag on the right. Use the existing Inter and system-sans stack for product titles, prices, sizes, and controls.

## Layout and interaction

Keep a two-column photo grid on phones and four columns at desktop width. Align the frames and give the 80s prints a deeper lower border. Keep original seller photos fully visible inside the frame. Use the existing Resonate logo in the header. Give the shop its own line beneath the size, with a small storefront icon.

Decade links filter the catalog by seller date clues and preserve the selected decade when searching or switching shelves. All decades leaves room for substantial 2000s pieces that meet the catalog’s quality criteria. Other collection controls refine the results. Local pickup is an optional, collapsed search that uses a ZIP only after submission. Seller names appear on cards without pickup addresses. The Goodwill pants shelf uses shippable listings and links to ShopGoodwill’s own search for its separate inventory. A card opens the seller-photo gallery, where thumbnails and zoom precede the link to the original item. The paper price tag is the playful motion detail. It swings once on pointer hover or keyboard focus and stays still with reduced motion. Email delivery is an explicit opt-in from the signed-in account. Fresh finds opens with live listings and guest filters. Email verification is available when a shopper chooses to save sizing and preferences.
