# Fieldwear Scout design

## North Star

An orderly workwear catalog with the personality of instant prints and hand-marked shop tags. Put the garment, fit, price, and shop in view before asking the shopper to open details.

## Color and type

The Scout stylesheet owns the runtime palette. White paper, charcoal ink, and navy controls define the browsing surface. Warm paper, muted brass, and soft gray are reserved for photo frames, tags, and email signup. Use the existing Inter and system-sans stack for product titles, prices, sizes, and controls.

## Layout and interaction

Keep a two-column photo grid on phones and four columns at desktop width. Align the frames and give each a deeper lower border. Keep original seller photos fully visible inside the frame. Use the existing Resonate logo in the header. Give the shop its own line beneath the size, with a small storefront icon.

Collections filter the same catalog. A card opens the seller-photo gallery, where thumbnails and zoom precede the link to the original item. The paper price tag is the playful motion detail. It swings once on pointer hover or keyboard focus and stays still with reduced motion. Email delivery is an explicit opt-in from the signed-in account.
