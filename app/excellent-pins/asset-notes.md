## Website Usage

- `hero-pin.jpg` - primary hero image; decorative premium pin example.
- `pin-soft-enamel.jpg` - colorful shaped enamel example; used for soft enamel.
- `pin-hard-enamel.jpg` - polished round color-and-metal pin; used for hard enamel.
- `pin-die-struck.jpg` - metal-only example; used for die struck.
- `pin-printed.jpg` - photo/detail style example; used for printed pins.
- `pin-event.jpg` - ribbon medal visual; used for medals.
- `pin-badge.jpg` - duplicate of the ribbon medal visual. Do not use this as a badge/nameplate example.
- `pin-custom-shape.jpg` - shaped character/emblem example. Do not use this as a keychain unless it is replaced with an actual keychain visual.
- `finishes.jpg` - metal finish sample image; used for "other / not sure" and finish explanation.
- `lovable-page/printed-pin.png` - Lovable printed pin card visual.
- `lovable-page/medal.png` - Lovable medal card visual.
- `lovable-page/novelty-coin.png` - Lovable novelty coin card visual.
- `lovable-page/badge-nameplate.png` - Lovable badge/nameplate card visual.
- `lovable-page/keychain.png` - Lovable keychain card visual.
- `lovable-page/not-sure-finishes.png` - Lovable finish comparison card visual.
- `lovable-page/finish-options-large.png` - Lovable customization choices finish visual.

## Notes

These are Lovable-generated visual examples, not final customer production photos. The page should not duplicate one visual across multiple product types just to fill a slot. The screenshot crops are acceptable for this current mockup pass because they restore the Lovable direction Kurtis approved, but they should be replaced with direct Lovable exports or Jack-approved product photos before final production.
